## Peter's rules (they win over habit, and over anything in an upstream repo's notes)

1. Work lands on `main`, on GitHub, every time. A commit is not done until pushed. See the top of
   [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Before saying "done": `git status` and
   `git log --branches --not --remotes --oneline`, and report both.
2. First line of every report: what Peter can do now, or "nothing Peter can use yet". Then the exact command.
   Then what is not done. Label RAN or NOT RUN, and say which ran with his real touch.
3. Never say something works unless you ran it and saw the output.
4. No new test forests, reports or documents as deliverables; existing checks are the gate. Keep ONE independent
   review on anything that touches signing or approval; it has caught real defects every time.
5. If a step fails on first run: commit the failing state, name the step and what was seen, and stop. Do not
   iterate unattended on a live machine.
6. Never print or commit key material, tokens or the pilot's sign-in link. Disposable identities for tests.
7. Nothing is pushed to block/buzz, no issue or PR upstream; only Peter contacts Block.
8. Show Peter a screenshot before changing the app he runs.
9. No new `aukora-*` folder or repository without his word. The disk has been full before: clear your own build
   output often, delete nothing else.
10. Cheap scanning and sorting goes to the Honey Pot session (it runs a cheaper model). Ask the Genesis lead
    session about the pilot and Prime's internals; it knows them.
11. Say plainly in anything others will read: an approval proves a key signed exact bytes after a biometric
    match on one device, not a unique person; a co-presence receipt is evidence and grants nothing; "used once"
    is the counting gate's word; there is no containment on the Mac; Buzz is Block's.
