## 9 Passkey login and seven word recovery

**Decision: passkey owner login, with no email or traditional-account requirement.** Internal owner identity records still bind credentials, scope and revocation. Disclose whether custody is provider-synced or device-bound. WebAuthn authenticators typically generate passkey keys; the WebAuthn API does not provide seed derivation or private-key export.
