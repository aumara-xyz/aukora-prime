# Owner approval: clarity with one click

Status: SOURCE; installed behavior must be verified by the operator after activation.

The owner chose clarity over forced friction. The card shows a gate-derived ROUTINE or CRITICAL label, a blue or red outline respectively, a plain “what this does” line, and gate-computed from→to facts. Model-authored text remains last, fenced and labelled. Approve remains one click: no typed codes, mandatory reveal, scroll-to-end or dwell.

Only the trusted theme-accent target is ROUTINE for a normal change. Plugin-set approval is CRITICAL even when its digest is unchanged. Reverts and unknown target specifications default to CRITICAL. Code, keys and egress are not newly allowed targets: this change expands no policy. The label describes scope; it grants no authority. A plugin-set receipt names the exact installed release and plugin set; launch admission still rechecks it.

The gate still binds the owner review to immutable proposal ID, base and new hashes, and a fresh expiring single-use challenge. Approval spends the proposal before writing, rechecks the base, applies stored bytes, checks the resulting hash and produces the existing signed receipt. Owner/proposer separation, release-floor protections and the decision argument grammar remain unchanged. The desktop validates the review and witnesses the rendered text and proposed bytes before enabling Approve. Clarity is read from that witnessed gate-facts section, never from model claims.

Synthetic previews and source checks do not prove a real owner decision or installed behavior. Both preview states require owner review before merge; integration and activation belong to the designated operator.
